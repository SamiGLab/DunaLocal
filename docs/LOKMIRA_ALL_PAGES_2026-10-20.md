# All localized detail pages

All 10 page types in HU and EN now use the light homepage design language: Services, Approach, About, Contact, Local SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Landing Pages, Social Media, Measurement.

Existing supplied service graphics are reused for Local SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Landing Pages and Measurement. Social Media uses an HTML editorial workflow because there is no corresponding supplied service graphic. Services, Approach, About and Contact use specific HTML panels with the pinned repository icons.

Body headings, cards, process blocks, FAQ accordions and bottom CTAs use shared spacing, type and color. About, Approach and Contact were rewritten to remove outdated pilot/PPF positioning and unapproved timing/contract promises. Unverified sector result stories were removed; actual service scope and deliverables remain. FAQs explain agreed scope, inputs, budget and evaluation without fixed-result promises.

URLs, reciprocal hreflang, sitemap, approved contact email, Google verification and indexing settings are retained. No new forms, trackers or external visual dependencies were added.
