FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How we begin our collaboration

Every engagement starts with a practical audit to ensure budget is only deployed into channels that deliver clear commercial value.

Should we start with local SEO or advertising?

Start by reviewing your Business Profile, key service pages and enquiry route. If those foundations are incomplete, improve them first. Where there is clear near-term demand and an approved budget, Google Ads may be a useful first test. The decision depends on intent, competition and your ability to handle enquiries.

When does a dedicated landing page make sense?

A new page may help when one high-value service needs a focused message that existing pages cannot communicate clearly. We review the current page before proposing additional development.

What does an initial assessment produce?

A prioritised list covering your offer, Budapest service area, Business Profile, relevant site pages and ways customers contact you. It is a planning output, not a requirement to buy every service listed here.

How do you tell clicks apart from actual enquiries?

A link click, a tracked form action and a qualified conversation are different events. Confirmed enquiries and booked work must be reconciled against business records where available.
