COORDINATED STRATEGY IN PRACTICE

How our capabilities connect.

Our six core areas do not operate in isolation. The strongest results occur when local intent, dedicated landing pages and rapid quote follow-up form one connected system. Here are three Budapest client scenarios.

STRATEGY 01 · BUILDING SERVICES

Emergency repairs and installations

Channels: Google Ads search campaigns + Conversion landing page + Call logging.

The workflow: A homeowner searching for urgent pipe repairs reaches a mobile landing page with an instant call button. The dispatcher logs caller location and requirements immediately.

Result: Minimal drop-off, predictable job bookings and zero wasted advertising spend on irrelevant search terms.

STRATEGY 02 · PRIVATE HEALTHCARE

Specialist clinic and aesthetic care

Channels: Google Business Profile local SEO + Social media showcases + Enquiry tracking.

The workflow: Budapest patients discover the practice on Maps with high-resolution clinic photos and verified reviews, while social channels reinforce trust before appointment booking.

Result: Higher patient confidence, lower no-show rates and resilient local market presence.

STRATEGY 03 · BESPOKE INTERIORS

Custom cabinetry and architecture

Channels: Meta lead campaigns + Pre-qualifying quote page + 14-day consultation cadence.

The workflow: High-intent Instagram portfolio browsers request estimates through a structured form specifying budget and room dimensions, followed by personal phone consultations.

Result: The sales team spends time exclusively with serious, pre-qualified homeowners.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How we begin our collaboration

Every engagement starts with a practical audit to ensure budget is only deployed into channels that deliver clear commercial value.

How do we choose which services to start with?

We review current search demand, existing digital assets and operational capacity. We always launch with the 1 or 2 channels that can generate measurable enquiries quickest.

Do we need all six services from day one?

Not at all. Most local businesses progress in phases: establishing search visibility and targeted campaigns first, before expanding into content and deeper pipeline tracking.

What advertising budget is required to get started?

For local Budapest services, a sensible monthly ad budget paired with disciplined campaign management provides statistically valid test results without unnecessary risk.

How quickly will we see measurable business results?

Google Ads and Meta campaigns generate immediate inbound enquiries within days, while Google Business Profile optimization and local SEO build sustained authority over 4 to 8 weeks.
