BOUNDARIES AND TRANSPARENCY

Technical framework and hosting

We do not build bloated or slow pages. Our landing pages are built with clean code, allowing them to sit smoothly on your existing domain or subdomain without altering your main website. The primary objective is eliminating wasted ad spend.

Frequently asked questions

What can the work include?

We plan the service message, page structure, mobile experience and next contact step.

What do you need from our business?

Accurate service information, target areas, existing website or profile links, relevant access and genuine business materials. We agree on the exact inputs before starting.

What budget should we plan for?

Budget depends on scope, competition, existing assets and your priorities. Service fees and any media spend are agreed separately.

How long will it take?

Timing depends on the work, access and approvals. We agree on a schedule and review points before implementation.

How will we review progress?

We agree on useful signals such as relevant traffic, contact actions, genuine enquiries and quotation follow-up. Available information and business feedback guide the review.

Can you guarantee business results?

No. Customer decisions and market conditions cannot be guaranteed. We agree on clear tasks and assess actual feedback rather than promise a fixed outcome.
