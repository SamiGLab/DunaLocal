Frequently asked questions

What monthly ad spend is recommended to start?

For local service businesses in Budapest, an initial test budget of 80,000 to 150,000 HUF per month is usually enough to identify which keywords generate genuine enquiries.

What is the difference between management fees and ad spend?

Ad spend is billed directly by Google for impressions and clicks, while our service fee covers campaign planning, setup, tracking and ongoing weekly optimisation.

How do we prevent wasted clicks?

We protect your budget using comprehensive negative keyword lists, tight geographic radiuses and excluding broad terms that only seek free information.

Why is a dedicated landing page important for Ads?

When visitors click an ad and land on a generic or cluttered homepage, they leave immediately. Directing ad clicks to a focused quotation page increases conversion significantly.
