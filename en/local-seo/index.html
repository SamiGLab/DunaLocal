BOUNDARIES AND TRANSPARENCY

Working parameters and ranking factors

Managing and optimising your Google Business Profile requires manager or owner access. We do not guarantee a fixed top ranking, and under no circumstances do we purchase or post artificial customer reviews. Local ranking is founded on three core pillars: relevance, distance, and prominence.

Frequently asked questions

What does a Business Profile audit check?

We review category relevance, opening hours, website and service links, listed offerings and potential duplicates. Findings are recorded as specific actions with rationale rather than a list of generic SEO suggestions.

What would a local keyword mapping example contain?

A simple worksheet links the search phrase and intent to the matching service page, existing coverage, and a recommended next step. District relevance and the actual service area are considered; rankings cannot be promised because distance and competition differ by searcher.

Should we put keywords into the business name?

No. The name on Google Business Profile should reflect the real business name rather than added marketing keywords. Relevant categories, genuine services and clear website content are safer, more useful improvements.

How can we assess local search progress?

We look at available Search and Maps visibility, meaningful profile actions and confirmed enquiries where the business can provide them. Impressions and direction requests are not the same as new customers.
