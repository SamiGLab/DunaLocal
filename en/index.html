COMMON QUESTIONS

Possibly not. We review the existing process first and recommend changes only when supported by evidence.

No. The pilot is a measurable market test. Outcomes depend on local demand, your capacity and competition.

No. Ad spend is billed separately. Service fees are agreed before work begins.
