THE METHOD

Three structured phases.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand demand.

Review the primary service, target area, current visibility and the points where potential customers reach out.

02 / PLAN

Run a focused pilot.

Define the tracked signals: search queries, enquiries, quotes and follow-up. Advertising spend is a separate cost.

03 / LEARN

Review actual outcomes.

Evaluate incoming enquiries and sent quotes together. We do not promise a guaranteed volume of bookings.

PROPOSED 90-DAY FORMAT

How the first 90 days are structured

Days 1-30 · Audit and foundations

Review the Business Profile, search opportunities, enquiry process and measurement options. Agree on priorities and a test plan.

Days 31-60 · Controlled pilot

Launch selected improvements and track incoming enquiry and quotation stages with the business.

Days 61-90 · Review

Assess what was measurable, what should be adjusted and whether to continue. This is a working framework, not a past client case study.

Clear boundaries

We do not promise guaranteed rankings, guaranteed revenue or fixed lead volumes. Pilot scope and fees are agreed upfront.

PRACTICAL BUDAPEST IMPLEMENTATION

Real-world execution in the 90-day cycle.

The pilot period is not abstract theory: we progress week by week with tangible deliverables matched to your real capacity and seasonal business patterns.

EXAMPLE 01 · DISTRICTS XI, XIII & XIV

Emergency plumbing & drainage

Days 1-30: Launch Google search ads targeted on emergency keywords and activate a mobile landing page with an instant call button.

Days 31-60: Eliminate irrelevant searches with negative keywords and establish a dispatcher call log.

Days 61-90: District breakdown review: concentrate ad budget on the fastest-to-reach Pest and Buda zones.

EXAMPLE 02 · DISTRICTS XI & XXII

Air conditioning & heat pumps

Days 1-30: Local search audit, build a pre-season landing page and set up a transparent quote request form.

Days 31-60: Launch Google Ads with survey booking and a strict 24-hour callback protocol.

Days 61-90: Quote closing rate evaluation: assess what percentage of issued proposals resulted in signed installations.

EXAMPLE 03 · DISTRICTS II & XII

Private specialist medical clinic

Days 1-30: Complete Google Business Profile cleanup, align operating hours and add authentic clinic photography.

Days 31-60: Optimize patient intake landing page and support direct online appointment scheduling.

Days 61-90: Review patient feedback trends and Google review acquisition to solidify local medical reputation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Methodology and working terms

We work under transparent, accountable terms with no long-term lock-ins required before proving commercial value.

What happens when the 90-day pilot concludes?

We review the numbers together: incoming enquiries, quotation quality and return on spend. If both parties agree, we continue on a monthly management retainer; otherwise, all created assets and accounts remain 100% your property.

Which businesses benefit most from this structured approach?

Budapest service businesses and technical trades that already have active operations and phone capacity, but want predictable enquiry volume and disciplined quote follow-up.

Why do we avoid guaranteeing #1 rankings or fixed revenue?

Search algorithms and consumer decisions cannot be guaranteed honestly. We focus on controllable commercial drivers: precision targeting, fast and compelling websites, and disciplined quotation follow-up.

How do ongoing communication and reporting work during the pilot?

We provide weekly status updates and a detailed monthly review meeting, focusing on straightforward business metrics rather than confusing digital marketing jargon.
