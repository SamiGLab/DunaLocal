BOUNDARIES AND TRANSPARENCY

Working parameters and lead quality

Media spend paid to Meta is a separate advertising budget. The business needs a clear process for responding to enquiries. We agree on useful qualifying questions and review the quality of actual contacts.

Frequently asked questions

Should we use a lead form or send traffic to a website?

An instant form can reduce mobile friction, while a landing page can explain a complex offer in more detail. We choose based on how the business will qualify and follow up the resulting enquiries.

What material is needed for the creative?

An approved offer, accurate service details, target area and images you have the right to use. Illustrations cannot be presented as actual completed client work or fabricated testimonials.

What happens when lead quality is poor?

We distinguish useful enquiries from irrelevant contacts, review the message, audience and form questions, and adjust future tests. Raw lead volume alone does not establish business value.

Are Meta ad charges included?

No. The advertising budget is separate from creative and campaign management fees. Deliverables and approval rounds should be agreed before publishing.
