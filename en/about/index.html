CORE VALUES IN PRACTICE

Transparency and true partnership.

In local digital marketing, long-term success requires mutual confidence. The following three guiding principles underpin every engagement from strategy design to weekly execution.

VALUE 01 · ACCOUNT OWNERSHIP

100% client-owned advertising assets

Execution: Every Google Ads and Meta ad account, tracking pixel and container is created directly under your business. We never operate agency black boxes: you maintain complete administrator access to all data and settings at all times.

VALUE 02 · CAPACITY ALIGNMENT

Demand matched to operational capacity

Execution: Our goal is never to overwhelm your staff with unqualified lead volume. Campaign tempo is strictly tuned to the real-world calendar of your technicians, workshop bays and intake personnel.

VALUE 03 · VERIFIABLE PROOF

Publishing only substantiated outcomes

Execution: Every figure and performance statistic we cite is backed by rigorous enquiry logs and analytics. We avoid inflated claims and only share verified commercial facts.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions about Lokmira

Learn more about our agency operations, direct invoicing model and day-to-day client collaboration.

Who manages advertising spend and media invoicing?

Ad spend is billed directly by Google and Meta to your corporate payment card with compliant VAT invoices. Lokmira invoices solely for strategic management, build and campaign optimization fees.

What types of Budapest businesses do we work best with?

Budapest service businesses, clinical practices, specialized contractors and technical trades who deliver exceptional service quality and care about disciplined quote follow-up.

Why do we avoid restrictive long-term lock-in contracts?

We believe client partnerships should be sustained by delivered value, not binding legal clauses. Following the initial 90-day pilot, monthly retainers can be cancelled with 30 days notice at any time.

How do we protect confidential business and pricing data?

We sign mutual non-disclosure commitments with every client. Your pricing margins, quotation details and customer lists are kept strictly confidential and never disclosed to third parties.
