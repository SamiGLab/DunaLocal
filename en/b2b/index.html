FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about B2B services

How do you verify target company data?

We verify company activity through national business registries, active company websites, public financial filings, and professional executive profiles before conducting outreach.

Can you guarantee closed contracts or signed deals?

No. We guarantee diligent research, verified decision-maker identification, and structured outreach. Commercial terms, pricing negotiation, and final contract execution remain entirely between your company and the prospective buyer.

Which countries and languages are supported?

We support market intelligence and business development across Central and Western Europe, with core research capabilities in English, Hungarian, and regional European trading hubs.

What do you need from us to begin?

We need your product specifications, target customer profiles, preferred countries, sample pricing, and commercial presentation materials. We review these together during initial onboarding.
