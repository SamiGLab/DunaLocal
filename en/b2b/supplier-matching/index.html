WHAT THE SERVICE COVERS

How this advisory service is delivered.

Every step is structured around clear deliverables, verified information, and transparent status reporting.

01 / SPECIFICATION

Technical Requirements Mapping

Auditing technical drawings, material tolerances, minimum order quantities, and target unit cost thresholds.

02 / IDENTIFICATION

Manufacturer Auditing & Scouting

Scouting qualified manufacturing facilities across Central and Eastern Europe, verifying ISO/CE certifications.

03 / QUOTATIONS

RFQ Management & Sample Logistics

Issuing structured Requests for Quotation (RFQs), comparing unit rates, and coordinating test sample shipments.

PRACTICAL DELIVERABLES

Actionable materials you receive

Throughout the engagement, we deliver structured documentation and data your team can immediately deploy.

Core deliverables
  1. 01Supplier Audit Matrix: Detailed comparison of 10-25 audited production facilities with machinery capacity and lead times.
  2. 02RFQ Rate Comparison Dossier: Clear breakdown of unit costs, tooling charges, payment terms, and volume discount tiers.
  3. 03Commercial Procurement Summary: Final recommendation highlighting the top 2-3 supplier candidates with sample audit notes.
